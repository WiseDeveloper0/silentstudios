Communities & Lounges

Places you'll actually want to hang out.

  • Voice lounges in animated rooms: a campfire, a café, outer space, a rainy window

Be yourself

A profile that actually feels like you.

  • Animated avatar decorations and profile frames
  • Styled display names with custom fonts and gradients
  • Pocket Notes: a short status that fades after 24 hours
  • Link Spotify, Steam, PlayStation, AniList, Twitch and more
  • Show as Active, Sleep, Do Not Disturb, or appear offline

Private by design

Your conversations belong to you.

  • End-to-end encrypted messages, photos, voice notes and files
  • Your encryption keys stay on your device
  • Screenshot alerts, and chats hidden in the app switcher
  • Face ID lock and two-factor sign-in
  • Teen accounts with safer defaults
  • No ads, and your data is never sold
